About family guy

Family Guy is an animated sitcom created by Seth MacFarlane that follows the lives of the Griffin family in the fictional town of Quahog, Rhode Island. The family consists of bumbling father Peter, his patient wife Lois, awkward teenage daughter Meg, dim-witted son Chris, sarcastic baby Stewie with a genius-level intellect and a penchant for world domination, and their talking dog Brian, who acts as the voice of reason. The show is known for its irreverent humor, cutaway gags, and satirical take on pop culture, politics, and everyday life.
